## ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF INSTALLATION

The Collective will install, operate, and maintain the following on the Property:

1. One (1) Sovereign Cell unit approximately [4ft × 4ft × 8ft] in footprint, containing:
   - H55 modular battery storage array
   - Biochar/mycelium structural shell (non-toxic, biodegradable)
   - PicoCELA Wi-Fi mesh node (one antenna, non-penetrating mount)
   - Optional: algae-skin exterior panel (~2 sq. ft.)

2. No permanent structures. No trenching or underground work without separate written consent.

3. All electrical connections shall be either solar-direct or grid-tied with Owner's explicit written approval.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — TERM

This MOU shall be effective upon both parties' signatures and shall remain in force for a period of **twelve (12) months**, renewable by mutual written agreement. The initial grant performance period is six (6) months.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — OWNER'S RIGHTS AND PROTECTIONS

4.1 **No Cost to Owner.** The Collective bears all costs of installation, operation, maintenance, and removal. The Owner pays nothing.

4.2 **Benefits to Owner.** The Owner receives free Wi-Fi access from the installed mesh node for the duration of this MOU.

4.3 **Removal.** The Collective shall remove all installed equipment within thirty (30) days of MOU termination or upon Owner's written request with thirty (30) days notice. The site will be restored to its original condition.

4.4 **Insurance.** The Collective shall maintain general liability insurance of no less than $1,000,000 per occurrence naming the Owner as an additional insured. Certificate of insurance shall be provided upon Owner's request.

4.5 **No Liens.** The Collective shall not permit any mechanics' or materialmen's liens to attach to the Property arising from this MOU.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — GRANT COMPLIANCE

5.1 The Owner acknowledges that this project may be funded in whole or in part by a City of Cuyahoga Falls CDBG grant administered under HUD regulations (24 CFR Part 570).

5.2 The Owner consents to reasonable access by City of Cuyahoga Falls and HUD representatives for inspection purposes during the grant performance period.

5.3 The Owner certifies that the Property is located in a Census Tract qualifying for LMI Area Benefit under HUD's Low-Mod Summary Data, or that the Property is used in a manner that primarily benefits LMI persons.
