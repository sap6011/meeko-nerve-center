What's been blocked — by whom — since when
Mycorrhizal Networks in Agriculture
Blocked by: Industrial herbicides + tillage + monoculture
Since ~1940s
Glyphosate (Roundup) kills mycorrhizal fungi. Deep tillage physically severs the network. Monoculture removes the plant diversity the network needs to route between species. Result: dead soil that requires synthetic fertilizer to produce anything — creating permanent dependency on the companies that sell both the herbicide and the fertilizer.
Evidence: Zaller et al. (2014) Scientific Reports. Helander et al. (2012) Applied Soil Ecology. USDA NRCS Soil Health Division, 2021.
Unlock: No-till farming + cover crops + compost = network reconnects within 2-3 years. Biochar accelerates it. Every food forest we plant restores kilometers of planetary internet.
The Electromagnetic Spectrum Commons
Blocked by: ITU frequency auctions + national telecom monopolies
Since 1920s
Radio spectrum was privatized through national licensing regimes starting in the 1920s. Frequencies that are physically free to use were auctioned to telecoms for billions, who then charged communities for access to their own electromagnetic environment. 3 billion people remain unconnected — not because the physics doesn't work, but because the license is unaffordable.
Evidence: ITU 2023 Facts and Figures. Rhizomatica legal case, Mexico (2013). FCC Community Broadband proceedings.
Unlock: Rhizomatica cracked Mexico. AirJaldi cracked India. Every country can have a community telecom license tier. The physics was never locked — only the paperwork. SolarPunk has the evidence base to make this case to regulators everywhere.
Open-Source Seeds
Blocked by: Plant patents + terminator genes + corporate consolidation
Since 1980 (Diamond v. Chakrabarty)
The 1980 Supreme Court decision allowed patents on living organisms. By 2022, four companies (Bayer/Monsanto, Corteva, ChemChina/Syngenta, BASF) controlled 60% of the global seed supply. Terminator technology (GURT) makes seeds sterile after one generation — the open-source seed forcibly converted to a subscription service. Farmers who save seed from patented varieties face lawsuits.
Evidence: ETC Group. Seed Industry Structure report 2022. USDA Plant Variety Protection database. Bowman v. Monsanto (2013).
Unlock: Seed libraries. Open Source Seed Initiative (OSSI) — 700+ varieties pledged open-source. Navdanya (India) — 5,000 traditional varieties preserved. Svalbard Global Seed Vault. Every community seed library is a jailbreak.
Traditional Ecological Knowledge
Blocked by: Language death + colonial epistemology + biopiracy
Since colonization · accelerating
A language dies every two weeks. Simultaneously, pharmaceutical companies patent molecules from traditional medicine without compensation (neem, turmeric, quinoa, hoodia — all documented biopiracy cases). The knowledge is taken. The community gets nothing.
Evidence: UNESCO Endangered Languages Atlas. India TKDL (Traditional Knowledge Digital Library) — documented 1,000+ biopiracy attempts. Convention on Biological Diversity Nagoya Protocol.

LENR Research (Low Energy Nuclear Reactions)
Blocked by: 1989 media suppression after Fleischmann-Pons controversy
Suppressed 1989–2020s
After the cold fusion controversy of 1989, the field was publicly declared dead. But the US Navy (SPAWAR), SRI International, MIT, and later DARPA and ARPA-E continued research. In 2019, Google published findings of anomalous heat in nickel-hydrogen systems. ARPA-E funded LENR research in 2022. The public burial was not based on science — it was based on press coverage of a failed replication attempt.
Evidence: Storms (2007) The Science of Low Energy Nuclear Reaction. LENRIA. ARPA-E LENR program, 2022. Tanzella et al., US Navy SPAWAR research.
Unlock: FOIA requests for DoE LENR assessment documents. Support ARPA-E research transparency. The science is genuinely uncertain — but the suppression was real, and the question deserves honest investigation.
Terra Preta — Amazonian Permanent Soil Technology
Blocked by: Colonial destruction of the civilizations that built it
Lost ~1500s · being reconstructed
Pre-Columbian Amazonian civilizations built soil that is still fertile today — 2,000 years later. The technology (terra preta do índio — "dark earth of the Indians") incorporated biochar, bone, ceramic, and organic matter into a permanently productive substrate. The civilizations that built it were destroyed by European colonialism. The formula was partially lost. We are reconstructing it now through biochar and soil science research.
Evidence: Lehmann et al., Amazonian Dark Earths (2003). Mann, 1491: New Revelations of the Americas Before Columbus. International Biochar Initiative research database.
Unlock: International Biochar Initiative. Humbo Forest project (Ethiopia) already using biochar reforestation. Every community food forest can begin rebuilding permanent soil. The knowledge is reconstructed enough to act on now.

Geothermal Energy — The Planet's Own Reactor
Blocked by: Fossil fuel subsidy structure + lack of political will
Chronically underfunded
The earth's core is 5,500°C — a fusion reactor that will burn for another 5 billion years. Superhot Rock (SHR) geothermal can theoretically provide baseload power anywhere on earth by drilling to 3-5km depth. The technology works. Companies like Quaise Energy, Fervo, and Project InnerSpace are advancing it. It receives 0.3% of global clean energy investment despite near-unlimited potential.
Evidence: MIT Future of Geothermal Energy (2006). IEA Geothermal Technology Report 2023. Project InnerSpace roadmap. Fervo Energy commercial deployment data.
Unlock: Direct funding advocacy to ARPA-E, IRENA, and national energy ministries. Community geothermal cooperatives (Iceland, New Zealand already proved it). Ngawha Māori geothermal cooperative is in our knowledge base — it's already here.
