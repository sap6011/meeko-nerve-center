💡 Why Crypto?

Crypto donations have real advantages over fiat for humanitarian causes.

⚡
Instant Settlement
Transfers confirm in seconds or minutes — no 3–5 business day bank delays.
🌎
Globally Accessible
Send from any country. No bank account needed. No wire transfer limits.
💸
Low Fees
Lightning and Solana transactions cost fractions of a cent. More money reaches aid.
🔒
No Chargebacks
Crypto transactions are final. No reversed donations, no payment disputes.
🔧
Censorship-Resistant
No payment processor can block or freeze a crypto transfer.
📊
Fully Auditable
Every transaction is on-chain and permanent. Full public audit trail, forever.
