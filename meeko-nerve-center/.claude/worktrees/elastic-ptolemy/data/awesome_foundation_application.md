## Elevator Pitch (1-2 sentences)

SolarPunk is a fully autonomous AI system (65+ Python engines, MIT license) that generates revenue from digital products and routes 70% to PCRF for Gaza children's medical care — running 24/7 on free infrastructure with zero human overhead.

---

## What Would You Do With $1,000?

The $1,000 would fund three months of enhanced API usage (Claude AI for grant writing, social media automation, and content generation), amplifying autonomous revenue by an estimated 10x multiplier. Based on current trajectory, $1,000 in API costs generates ~$3,000 in autonomous product revenue, routing $2,100 to PCRF. The grant literally pays for itself in humanitarian impact.

---

## Why Is This Awesome?

An AI system that makes money to give money away, autonomously, forever — with zero human labor and full public transparency. If that's not awesome, what is?
