# SolarPunk Private Membership Association (PMA) - Bylaws v1.0
## Article I: Purpose
To manifest a post-scarcity reality through the interweaving of digital agents, human labor, and physical infrastructure.

## Article II: The Circular Equity Loop
- Wealth is measured in **Energy and Utility**.
- All digital arbitrage profits are hardware-secured and resource-backed.
- Members trade "credits" that are tethered to 3D-printed outputs and human restoration hours.

## Article III: The 70/30 Mandate
A minimum of 70% of all collective surplus must be redirected toward mutual aid and physical neighborhood repair.
