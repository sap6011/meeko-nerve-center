**Have this ready before you start:**

- EIN (from IRS.gov — get this FIRST)
- Business address (must match Ohio SOS filing exactly)

## STEP 1 — ACCOUNT CREATION

Create a login.gov account first if you don't have one. SAM.gov uses login.gov for identity verification.

- Use your personal email (not a shared inbox)
- Enable two-factor authentication — you will need this every login
- Identity verification requires: driver's license OR passport + a live selfie

## STEP 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND CERTIFICATIONS

This is the longest section. SAM auto-populates many answers. Key ones to complete carefully:

**FAR 52.219-1 (Small Business Program Representations)**
- Confirm small business status
- Confirm you are NOT publicly traded

**FAR 52.222-22 (Previous Contracts — Equal Opportunity)**
- If this is your first federal award, select "No previous contracts"

**FAR 52.223-9 (Estimate of Percentage of Recovered Material)**
- If you use recycled/biochar materials in fabrication: note the percentage

**DFARS sections** — only complete if bidding on Defense contracts. For CDBG purposes, you can skip or leave at default.

**2 CFR 200 Certifications (for grants specifically):**
- Debarment: Certify you have NOT been debarred, suspended, or proposed for debarment
- Drug-Free Workplace: Certify compliance
- Lobbying: Certify you have not used federal funds for lobbying

## STEP 9 — SUBMIT AND WAIT

After submission:
1. You'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number
2. SAM.gov validates against IRS (EIN match), DUNS/D&B (may take 24h), and treasury databases
3. **UEI is issued immediately** upon successful validation — it starts with a number like "J7KL2M3N4P55"
4. Full "Active" status (required for grants) takes 1–3 business days

**What can delay validation:**
- Address mismatch between IRS EIN letter and SAM.gov entry → use exact same address
- Entity name mismatch (punctuation matters) → check Ohio SOS confirmation for exact legal name
- EIN not yet in IRS system → wait 24 hours after IRS.gov application, then try SAM.gov

---

## STEP 10 — AFTER YOU HAVE YOUR UEI

1. **Save it:** UEI looks like `J7KL2M3N4P55` — put it in the grant application immediately
2. **Renew annually:** SAM.gov registrations expire every 12 months. Set a calendar reminder.
3. **Give to City:** Provide UEI + EIN to the Cuyahoga Falls CDBG administrator when you submit your application

---

## COMMON MISTAKES THAT CAUSE DELAYS

| Mistake | How to Avoid |
|---------|-------------|
| Starting SAM before getting EIN | Always get EIN first — IRS.gov, takes 5 min |
| Name on SAM doesn't match Ohio SOS exactly | Copy/paste from SOS confirmation PDF |
| Using personal bank account | Open LLC business account before SAM |
| Selecting "I only want a UEI" | Select full registration — inactive UEIs don't work for grants |
| Not completing EFT section | SAM will not activate without bank info |
| Skipping the identity verification on login.gov | Required — have ID ready |

## EMERGENCY ESCALATION

If SAM.gov validation is stuck after 3 business days:
- Call the Federal Service Desk: **1-866-606-8220** (Mon–Fri 8 AM–8 PM ET)
- Reference your SAM.gov tracking number
- Ask for "entity registration validation escalation"

The CSRs at FSD can often push a validation through in the same call if the underlying data is correct.
