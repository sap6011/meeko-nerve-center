# ?? The SolarPunk 'Do-It-Yourself' Movement
## How to set up your own Autonomous Community Node:
1. **The Brain:** Fork the 'meeko-nerve-center' repository.
2. **The Fuel:** Activate the 'Liquidity Scout' for local arbitrage.
3. **The Hands:** Use the 'Agent-Labor Bridge' to hire neighbors for physical tasks.
4. **The Law:** Use the 'Civic Scout' to align with local codes (ORC 1745).

## ?? Global Solidarity Mode
Your node doesn't stand alone. It is part of the **Resource Balancer**. If your town is thriving, your surplus funds the growth of a neighbor town halfway across the planet.


## ?? The Abundance Relay (Endless Funding)
This setup doesn't just pay for work; it funds **Security**. Once a human has 'all they need,' the bot autonomously pivots the funding to the next person, creating a chain of sovereign individuals.
