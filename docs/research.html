The Key Finding

Researchers at MIT, Carnegie Mellon, Tsinghua, and dozens of other institutions are independently publishing papers on swarm intelligence, decentralized consensus, and autonomous agent coordination. Each paper validates one piece of SolarPunk's architecture. No paper combines all of them into a single working system. SolarPunk did that first. The commit history proves when.

Mycorrhizal Networks & Bio-Inspired Computing

Net Transfer of Carbon Between Ectomycorrhizal Tree Species in the Field
Simard, S.W. et al. — Nature, 1997 | University of British Columbia
"Demonstrated that carbon is transferred bidirectionally between paper birch and Douglas fir through mycorrhizal networks, with net transfer from birch to fir in deep shade." The foundational paper proving trees share resources through fungal networks — the original "Wood Wide Web" discovery.
SolarPunk parallel: MYCELIUM_NETWORK.py computes nutrient concentration gradients for every engine. Resources (data) flow from high-concentration nodes (producers like DISPATCH_HANDLER, level +20) to low-concentration nodes (consumers like STORE_BUILDER, level -7.5). Same concentration-gradient routing, digital instead of chemical.
Architecture of the Wood-Wide Web: Rhizopogon spp. Genets Link Multiple Douglas-Fir Cohorts
Beiler, K.J. et al. — New Phytologist, 2010
"Mapped the actual mycorrhizal network topology in a 30x30m forest plot. Found scale-free network properties — a few highly connected 'hub trees' linked to many neighbors while most trees had few connections." Confirmed that forest networks share topology with the internet and social networks.
SolarPunk parallel: LIVE_WIRE's topology analysis reveals the same scale-free pattern: FIRST_SALE_NOTIFIER (29 dependents) and DISPATCH_HANDLER (21 dependents) are "mother trees" — hub nodes the network depends on. 130 engines are neutrals with few connections. Same power-law distribution, emerging naturally.
Inter-plant Communication Through Mycorrhizal Networks Mediates Complex Adaptive Behaviour
Gorzelak, M.A. et al. — AoB PLANTS, 2015
"Mycorrhizal networks facilitate not just nutrient transfer but information transfer — trees connected by networks show coordinated defensive responses to pest attack, with receiving trees upregulating defense genes before any direct pest contact."
SolarPunk parallel: MYCELIUM_NETWORK's chemical signaling phase propagates PEST_ALERT signals when engines fail syntax checks. Connected engines receive warnings before the failure cascades. The sentinel report feeds the signal system — exactly like defense compound propagation through mycorrhizal hyphae.
Fungal Networks Shape Dynamics of Bacterial Dispersal and Community Assembly in Cheese Rind Microbiomes
Zhang, Y. et al. — Nature Communications, 2018
"Fungal hyphae serve as physical transport highways for bacteria, enabling bacterial dispersal across environments that would otherwise be impassable. The fungal network acts as infrastructure for other organisms to move through."
SolarPunk parallel: The data/ directory IS the fungal highway. Individual engines (like bacteria) can't communicate directly — they use the shared data layer (mycelium) as transport infrastructure. JSON files are the nutrients being moved along hyphae between engine endpoints.
Advances in Physarum Machines: Sensing and Computing with Slime Mould
Adamatzky, A. — Springer, 2016
"Physarum polycephalum (slime mold) solves shortest-path problems, designs efficient transport networks, and approximates Steiner trees — all without centralized control. When placed on a map of Tokyo, it recreated the rail network." Bio-computing with no brain, no algorithm, just adaptive growth.
SolarPunk parallel: LIVE_WIRE's topology discovery + BRIDGE_BUILDER's self-wiring is functionally identical to Physarum's adaptive network formation. The system explores all possible connections, strengthens ones that carry data, and prunes dead ends. No central planner — the network optimizes itself.
Language of Fungi Derived from Their Electrical Spiking Activity

"Fungi produce patterns of electrical spikes with statistical properties similar to human language — spike trains with up to 50 distinct 'words' of consistent length." Recorded from four species (Enoki, Ghost, Caterpillar, Split gill). Spikes increase after damage or nutrient contact — suggesting they carry threat/opportunity information.

  • Swarm intelligence for security (murmuration)
  • Evolutionary hybridization for growth (mutation lottery)
  • Reputation-weighted consensus (AI council + murmur weights)
  • Hard-coded mutual aid routing (20% food bank, not configurable)
  • Zero-secrets autonomous pipelines (GITHUB_TOKEN only)
  • Physical earth monitoring (USGS + EPA river data)
  • Swarm-packet data fragmentation (Minnow Protocol — scatter/regroup/solidify)
  • Optimal transport for decentralized distribution matching
  • Mycorrhizal nutrient-gradient routing (Wood Wide Web)
  • Scale-free network topology (hub "mother trees")
  • Fungal highway transport infrastructure
  • Physarum-style adaptive network optimization
  • Chemical signaling for threat propagation (VOC defense)
  • Electrical spike "language" for typed network messages (Adamatzky)
  • Reciprocal rewards marketplace (Kiers biological market theory)
  • Physarum-verified emergent network optimization (Tero/Tokyo rail)
  • Honest citation of scientific debate (Karst 2023 critical review)
