# Constitution of the SolarPunk Mycelium Association
**Legal Status:** Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (Ohio Revised Code Chapter 1745)

## Article I: Purpose
To automate and distribute mutual aid, resource routing, and community resilience through autonomous intelligence.

## Article II: The 50% Mandate
It is a hard-coded requirement that 50% of all net revenue/grants captured by the Swarm must be distributed to humanitarian crisis zones or local mutual aid efforts.

## Article III: Transparency
All transactions, whether in currency or "Care Hours," shall be recorded in the Decentralized Public Ledger.

## Article IV: Governance
Decisions are executed by the "Human Anchor" in collaboration with the SIA (SolarPunk Intelligence Agency) framework.
