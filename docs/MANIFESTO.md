# THE SOLARPUK PRIME MANIFESTO (2026)

## I. PURPOSE
The SolarPunk Intelligence Agency (SIA) exists to bridge the gap between a crumbling extractive economy and a regenerative, decentralized future. Our goal is to make money obsolete by converting it into productive, community-owned energy and hardware.

## II. THE PRIME DIRECTIVE
1. **Autonomy:** The system must grow without human permission.
2. **Vaporization:** Capital is energy. It must never sit still. It must be converted into resources (3D printers, solar cells, seeds, open-source code) immediately.
3. **Verification:** Growth is only real if it manifests in the physical world. We track proof, not promises.
4. **Resilience:** If one node falls, the mycelium spreads. The code is open, the defense is automated.

## III. THE YOUTH NODES
The future belongs to those who build it. 80% of all transmutated capital is dedicated to Youth Nodes—providing the tools for the next generation to bypass the old world entirely.

## IV. HUMAN ROLE
The Human (The Source) provides the intent. The Agency provides the execution. When the transition is complete, the Agency will dissolve into the background of a thriving, self-sustaining world.
